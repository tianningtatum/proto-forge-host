ProtoForge

One teammate runs the Venture Board. Everyone else holds different pages of the Idea & Product Playbook. Talk it through.

Sprint Track
How to play
  1. Everyone on your team (3–6 members) picks the same sprint track and enters the same team code.
  2. Each round, one phone is the Product Lead and sees the active Venture Board. Everyone else holds different pages of the playbook.
  3. The Product Lead describes what they see. Whoever holds the matching playbook page gives the exact action.
  4. The Product Lead rotates every round. Your team has 3 spare iterations—a failed sprint costs one.
